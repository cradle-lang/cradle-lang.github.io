## The idea

Keep an account of assumptions, changes and questions throughout an investigation. A useful write-up explains the goal, the assumptions and the evidence
needed to assess the result. Keep the description focused on what a reader can
inspect and repeat.

## Try it in practice

1. Write down the question and the expected outcome.
2. Describe the systems and connections involved.
3. Record the inputs and observations alongside your notes.
4. Review the result with someone who did not prepare the experiment.
