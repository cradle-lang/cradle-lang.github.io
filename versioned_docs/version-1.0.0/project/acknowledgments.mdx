# Contributors to CRADLE

CRADLE has evolved through the work of researchers, developers, students, maintainers and collaborating organizations. This page recognizes the people and organizations that have contributed to the research, development and continued evolution of CRADLE.

Before becoming an open-source project, CRADLE was designed and developed by its founding team as part of the original research effort. Their work established the language, supporting tools and technical foundations that continue to support the development of CRADLE today.

## Project leads
They provide direction for the project's research and development, including
its system design, technical development and continued work on reproducible
cyber experimentation.

## Research and Community Support

We would like to extend our sincere thanks to the researchers, institutions, infrastructure providers and wider community who have supported the research and development of CRADLE.

As CRADLE continues as an open-source project, we are equally grateful to the developers, researchers, users and community members who contribute code, documentation, feedback, testing, ideas and other forms of support. Every contribution helps strengthen and advance the project.
